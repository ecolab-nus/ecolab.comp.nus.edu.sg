PACE

PACE

28nm 2024 A*STAR IME

PACE integrates our HyCUBE CGRA into a full RISC-V SoC, targeting next-generation IoT edge computing. The chip features multi-hop routers and idle-state instructions for energy-efficient acceleration, achieving 360 GOPS/W. Developed in collaboration with the Institute of Microelectronics (A*STAR) and presented at Hot Chips 2024.

HyCUBE

HyCUBE

40nm 2019

HyCUBE is a power-efficient CGRA accelerator for IoT applications, featuring a reconfigurable single-cycle multi-hop interconnect that enables distant functional units to communicate in one clock cycle without occupying intermediate compute resources. The test chip operates at 0.9V, achieves a peak efficiency of 26.4 MOPS/mW with only 290 pJ per operation, and runs at a maximum frequency of 753 MHz with 4 hops per cycle. It delivers 28.6x and 26.5x better power efficiency compared to Xilinx Zynq FPGA and ARM Cortex-A7, respectively.

Stitch

Stitch

65nm 2018

Stitch is a heterogeneous many-core processor for next-generation wearable devices. It features 16 cores arranged in a mesh network, where each core contains a tiny accelerator patch that can be "stitched" together virtually to form large, application-specific accelerators. This hardware-software co-design approach achieves 2x better performance while being 7.5x smaller than its predecessor LOCUS. Tested across finger gesture recognition, image classification, and indoor localization tasks. Presented at ISCA 2018.

LOCUS

LOCUS

65nm 2016

LOCUS (Low-Power Customizable Many-Core Architecture) is a 16-core message-passing processor designed for ultra-low-power wearable devices. Each tile combines an open-source ARM Amber core with a Special Functional Unit (SFU) for domain-specific acceleration, connected by a customizable network-on-chip. LOCUS achieves 3.1x performance-per-watt improvement over quad-core ARM processors found in commercial wearables. It was the first silicon tapeout from ECO Lab and the foundation for the Stitch architecture.
